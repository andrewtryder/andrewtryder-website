Background · Perspective

About Andrew Ryder

I have a broad background in technology, spanning software development, infrastructure, networking, IT operations, and technical leadership.

Over the years, I've worked in a variety of roles, including Cofounder/CTO and Head of Technology. But I've always enjoyed being directly involved in the work—whether that's troubleshooting a difficult technical problem, building an application, maintaining systems, or helping someone get technology working the way it should.

More recently, I've been spending a lot of time working with AI. I use it in my own development workflows and continue exploring how language models, automation, and integrations can make software more useful and solve practical problems. I'm particularly interested in understanding both what these tools can do and where their limitations matter.

Across all of this, what I enjoy most is learning how things work, solving problems, and building things that are genuinely useful.

My experience covers software, systems, infrastructure, support, and the technologies that connect them.

AI, Automation & Integrations

I work with AI-assisted development, language models, APIs, and automation to build and improve practical tools. My interests include integrating AI into existing workflows, evaluating its output, and understanding where it can reliably reduce manual work.

Education & Current Focus
