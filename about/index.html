About Andrew Ryder

A hands-on technologist focused on pragmatic software development, resilient infrastructure, and solving everyday operational problems.

I enjoy figuring out how things work from first principles, diagnosing why they fail, and building tools that make daily life and work smoother.

My experience spans full-stack software development, systems engineering, Linux and Windows administration, Mac desktop support, networking, and IT operations. Over the course of my career—including roles as a Cofounder/CTO and Head of Technology—I have worn many hats, but my core instinct has always been that of a builder and troubleshooter rather than an administrator of corporate abstractions.

I believe technology is at its best when it is quiet, reliable, and respectful of the person using it. Whether that means untangling an intricate database constraint, tracking down an intermittent network packet loss, writing a low-level driver to interface with physical hardware, or streamlining an internal tool so colleagues can get their work done with less friction, I care deeply about the details that create trust and longevity in systems.
