Craft · Physical Mediums

Creative Practice

An ongoing exploration of physical materials, composition, and craftsmanship through photography and metal fabrication.

Working with physical materials provides a grounding counterbalance to writing code. In a workshop or while making photographs, the feedback is direct: each choice leaves a visible trace.

Both disciplines demand patience, strict attention to tolerances, and respect for constraints.

Visual Arts

Photography

Photography is a personal practice centered on composition, light, and the character of ordinary places.

I value the deliberate attention it asks of framing, light, and tonal relationships.

Metal Sculpture & Fabrication

Metalwork is another way to think through form, structure, and material by hand.

The practice includes functional shop work and small sculptural experiments, with an interest in balance, proportion, and honest finishes.

Materials and techniques vary by project.
