Craft · Physical Mediums

Creative Practice

An ongoing exploration of physical materials, composition, and craftsmanship through photography and metal fabrication.

Working with physical materials provides a grounding counterbalance to writing code. In a workshop or darkroom, feedback is immediate and immutable: steel either holds or warps under heat; a silver gelatin print reveals every choice made in exposure and chemistry.

Both disciplines demand patience, strict attention to tolerances, and respect for constraints.

Visual Arts

Photography

My photography focuses on structural geometry, industrial remnants, and austere New England landscapes. I work primarily in black and white using both medium-format film and digital workflows.

The deliberate pace of analog photography encourages careful consideration of framing, light quality, and tonal gradients before the shutter opens. In the darkroom, the craft is physical—managing developer timing, paper contrast grades, and archival washing to produce prints intended to last.

Fabrication · Shop

Metal Sculpture & Fabrication

In the shop, I work primarily with steel, stainless alloys, and titanium. My projects range from custom workshop tooling and structural fixtures to minimalist geometric sculptures.

I specialize in manual TIG (Gas Tungsten Arc) welding, where arc stability, heat input, and shielding gas flow dictate the mechanical strength and visual character of each weld bead. Sculptural pieces explore balance, positive and negative volume, and raw, mechanically finished metallic surfaces without decorative coatings.

Materials & Techniques: Steel · Titanium · TIG Welding · Cold Sawing · Surface Conditioning · Fixture Design.
