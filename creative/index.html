Photography

A personal collection exploring observation, composition, light, and everyday places.

Photography is a way for me to slow down and pay attention. I enjoy the process of noticing a scene, finding an interesting composition, and seeing how light can change the way something familiar looks.

What appeals to me most is the act of observation. Framing an image means deciding what to include, what to leave out, and when a moment feels worth capturing. It's a personal pursuit that gives me a different way to explore the world outside of my work in technology.
